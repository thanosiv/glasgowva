Town highlights

  • Historic community nestled in the Blue Ridge foothills along the James and Maury rivers.
  • Open, accessible local government dedicated to community service.
  • A welcoming home for residents, local businesses, and outdoor recreation.

About Glasgow

Small-town character with a clear civic purpose.

Community overview

Glasgow, Virginia was settled as early as the 1730s and remains a compact community shaped by its natural environment and riverfront setting.

Nearby attractions

The town is a short drive from Natural Bridge State Park and close to historic downtown Lexington, creating a strong mix of local life and regional access.
