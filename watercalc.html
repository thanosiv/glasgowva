Water and Sewer Calculator

Enter your current usage value to estimate monthly charges for Town of Glasgow and Rockbridge County PSA.

This calculator is intended to help residents compare the cost of Town of Glasgow water and sewer service with the Rockbridge County PSA rates, as part of understanding the potential impact of surrendering our charter and reincorporating with Rockbridge County.

Current published rates

Town of Glasgow water and sewer rates shown below are taken from the News-Gazette's May 20, 2026 edition.

This calculator was created in cooperation with the Rockbridge County PSA.

Town of Glasgow

  • Water: $38.00 base + $5.40 per 1,000 gallons over 3,000 gallons
  • Sewer: $42.00 base + $2.80 per 1,000 gallons over 3,000 gallons

Rockbridge County PSA

  • Water: $8.60 per 1,000 gallons
  • Sewer: $13.85 per 1,000 gallons
  • Water account charge: $13.56 ($27.12 every 2 months)
  • Sewer account charge: $20.84 ($41.67 every 2 months)

While the PSA bills every 2 months, the value calculated is broken down to monthly to provide a clear comparison.

Trash pickup is not included by the county, and would constitute an additional expense for residents.
